# Dylan Roy

I’m building an open, AI-assisted mathematics research workspace focused on Gaussian random fields, persistent homology and the geometry of short-lived topological features.

The aim is to make the work inspectable: full arguments, exact-source references, reproducible computations and clearly recorded review scopes.

[Explore the public site](https://d6g8k5htny-coder.github.io/main/site/) · [Try a reproducible example](https://d6g8k5htny-coder.github.io/main/site/reproduce.html) · [Cite the work](https://d6g8k5htny-coder.github.io/main/site/cite.html)

## Research approach

The work combines human direction, AI-assisted derivation, adversarial review, exact computation and selected Lean 4 formalizations. Results have specific hypotheses and review histories; those scopes travel with each artifact. “Universal Law” names the research workspace, not a claim that a universal law has been discovered.

For the longer technical account and research background, read the [research stack essay](docs/RESEARCH_STACK.md), preserved from the previous profile.
